Questions

Before you buy.

Which Excel version do I need?

Excel 2016 or later on Windows or Mac, or Google Sheets. Every formula is standard: no macros, no add-ins, no external links. In Google Sheets use File → Import → Replace spreadsheet.

Why does the workbook arrive filled in?

Every workbook ships pre-filled with the same fictional plant so each dashboard, flag and trend has something to show. Read it that way first, then clear the yellow input cells; the white computed cells empty themselves.

Do the tools follow ISO 9001?

They are built around the clauses a quality manager runs day to day: 9.2 internal audit, 10.2 nonconformity and corrective action, 8.4 external providers, 9.3 management review, and 6.1 risk. They reference clause numbers as pointers only and reproduce no text from the standard.

What is the difference between an entry worksheet and a pack?

An entry worksheet is two or three tabs lifted from its pack, for $9 to $12. The pack is the full workbook with the register, the dashboard and the field guide PDF that explains the method, for $35. If you buy the worksheet and then the pack, email for the difference back.

Gumroad or Etsy?

The same files at the same prices. Gumroad delivers updates to past buyers and runs the discount codes from the videos; Etsy is there for buyers who prefer it. Buy wherever you already have an account.

Can I use them across a team or a company?

One purchase covers one plant or site, any number of users at that site. Consultants using the tools with clients, and groups with several sites, should email for a licence.

What if it does not do what the page says?

Thirty days, no questions. Reply to the receipt email and it is refunded.
