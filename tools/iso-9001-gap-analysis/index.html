The method

A modest gap on a requirement that changed outranks a large gap on one that did not.

Every certification body publishes a free ISO 9001 gap analysis checklist. They are all the same document: a question per clause and a comments column. They identify gaps and stop at exactly the point the work begins — because the work is deciding which gaps matter, and a checklist cannot decide anything.

A gap analysis for the 2026 transition has to make one judgment a flat list cannot: a modest gap on a requirement that changed outranks a large gap on one that did not. Your auditor will spend the transition audit on the changes — quality culture and ethical behavior, the separated handling of risks and opportunities, the strengthened change planning, the renumbered clause 10 — and a system that is 95% conforming on unchanged clauses and 40% on the changed ones is not 90% ready. It is exposed exactly where the audit will look.

So score every sub-clause, weight the score by the change type, rank the result, and take the top of the list to the action plan with a control level attached — because a transition "implemented" entirely through procedures and training is a transition documented, not done.
