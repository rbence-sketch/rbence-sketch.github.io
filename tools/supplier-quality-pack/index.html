The method

Supplier control that runs on data instead of the last bad experience.

Supplier evaluation with a quality override: a supplier cannot score its way past a quality failure on price and delivery. The approved supplier list carries expiry flags, so re-evaluation happens on a date and not after a problem.

The performance log feeds a 60/40 scorecard, with on-time delivery measured against the original promised date rather than the last revised one. Issue types are logged beside rejects, so a pattern shows before the reject count does.

The SCAR register escalates on its own: ESCALATE appears when a poor rating meets an open corrective action request. The re-evaluation tab closes the loop, and the 2026 edition's new question under 8.4.3, how the provider treats your customers, has a place in the evaluation.

Which Excel version do I need?

Excel 2016 or later on Windows or Mac, or Google Sheets. Every formula is standard: no macros, no add-ins, no external links. In Google Sheets use File → Import → Replace spreadsheet.

Every workbook ships pre-filled with the same fictional plant so each dashboard, flag and trend has something to show. Read it that way first, then clear the yellow input cells; the white computed cells empty themselves.
