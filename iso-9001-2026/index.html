ISO 9001:2026: what changed for your plant.

The sixth edition publishes on 16 September 2026 and every 2015 certificate has to transition before the window closes. The reported change areas.

For each one, pick the answer that describes what an auditor could find in your system this week, not what you intend to write.

4. Quality culture

Reported: the new edition makes quality culture explicit, with leadership expected to shape and show it.

5. Ethical behavior

Reported: ethical behavior becomes explicit inside the quality system rather than living only in HR policy.

6. Leadership in continual improvement

Reported: leadership's role in driving improvement is strengthened, beyond signing the management review minutes.

7. Risks and opportunities

Reported: clearer wording on how risks and opportunities are determined, actioned and evaluated.

8. Climate change in context

Already required by the 2024 amendment to the 2015 edition and expected to carry into 2026: the context review considers whether climate change is a relevant issue.

9. Terms and definitions

Reported: more terms are defined inside the standard itself. Where your manual defines the same terms differently, an auditor will ask which one you run.
